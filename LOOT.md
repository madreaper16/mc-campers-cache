# Default loot

Camp supplies include gunpowder,
gold nuggets, emeralds, bones, a chance of a saddle, and one diamond item with
Unbreaking II and Curse of Vanishing. Camp ownership and restitution still apply.

| Reward | Supply shack | Hidden storehouse (entire double chest) |
|---|---|---|
| Diamond gear | Guaranteed 1–2 pieces | Guaranteed 3–4 pieces, plus possible God Armor |
| Armor enchantments | Protection II, Unbreaking II; boots also Feather Falling II | Protection III, Unbreaking III; boots also Feather Falling III |
| Pickaxe/axe enchantments | Efficiency II, Unbreaking II | Efficiency IV, Unbreaking III |
| Sword enchantments | Sharpness II, Unbreaking II | Sharpness IV, Unbreaking III |
| Enchanted books | Guaranteed 1–2 | Guaranteed 2–3, at higher levels |
| Valuables | 3–4 draws | 6–9 draws |
| Cooked food | 1–2 draws | 2–4 draws, larger stacks |
| Useful materials | 2–4 draws | 5–8 draws, larger stacks |
| Experience bottles | — | Guaranteed 8–16 |
| Netherite scrap | — | 25% chance of 1–2 |
| God Armor | — | 1.5% chance, once per storehouse |

Normal gear is undamaged, with compatible enchantments and no
curses, Fire Protection, Thorns, or other filler enchantments. Types are randomly
selected from helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, pickaxe, axe, and sword; repeated
types are possible.

Books each contain one useful enchantment: Protection, Unbreaking, Efficiency,
Sharpness, Fortune, Looting, Feather Falling, Power, or Mending. Mending has weight
1 and the others weight 3. Shack levels are III, II, III, III, II, II, III, III, I
respectively; storehouse levels are IV, III, IV, IV, III, III, IV, IV, I.

Valuables draw from iron ingots, diamonds, emeralds and gold ingots. Materials draw
from torches, coal, oak/spruce logs, glass, cobblestone, gunpowder, leather, string
and arrows. Food draws from cooked beef, porkchops, chicken and baked potatoes.
Individual types are chance rewards; every site guarantees its gear and books.

The named **God Armor** diamond chestplate has exactly Unbreaking III, Mending I,
and Protection V. Its single 1.5% roll is assigned to only one half of the double
chest. The other half contains extra supplies, with no additional rare roll.
Opening order does not affect eligibility. Ordinary storehouse armor does not
roll Protection V or Mending.

The destination chests use native Minecraft loot tables and generate once, on
first access or destruction, with no refills. Loot works in naturally generated
structures and newly placed test structures. Previously generated empty chests
are not retrofitted; inspect newly generated structures or a fresh test world.
Loot tables are added when the mod resources are built. Map trading is still in development.

Verification covers actual template chest NBT, both double-chest halves, first
access and no-refill behavior, gear/book guarantees, curse-free enchantments,
Protection V preservation, chest capacity, and 20,000 Minecraft loot rolls.
